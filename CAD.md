# CAD downloads

## V1 · SolidWorks

[Download the V1 SolidWorks archive](v1-solidworks-cad.zip). Open `previewAssembly.SLDASM` with the companion files in the same folder. `CHAP_V0_Joint.SLDPRT` and `CHAP_V0_Thumb.SLDPRT` are the separately printed hardware-fit revisions used on the prototype. They were never inserted into the reference assembly. `My_ElectronicsCaseBase.SLDPRT` is the modified holder.

The reference parts are derived from [laxmangordon's Third Thumb Project](https://github.com/laxmangordon/Third-Thumb-Project). The ZIP includes the original [MIT license notice](https://github.com/laxmangordon/Third-Thumb-Project/blob/main/LICENSE).

## V2 · STEP

[Download the V2 STEP archive](v2-step-cad.zip). It contains 28 unique part bodies for the revision 23 bench assembly, including the repair 17 yaw rotor. Import the individual STEP files into your CAD system. Dimensions are in millimeters. The [assembled CAD view](v2-assembly-cad.jpg) shows their relative positions. The remote servos, tubes, and braid are illustrated separately in the [yaw](v2-yaw-tendon-routing.jpg) and [curl](v2-curl-tendon-routing.jpg) routing references.

P09 and P10 use the same geometry. The retired S07 spacer is omitted. The archive contains part geometry and the assembly view is a separate image.
